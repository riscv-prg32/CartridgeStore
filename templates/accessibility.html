Accessibility statement

{{ store_name }} aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, the level referenced by the European standard EN 301 549.

What we do

  • Every page has a skip link, labelled landmarks, one main heading and a logical heading order.
  • All controls work with a keyboard and show a visible focus indicator; menus are native disclosure widgets.
  • Form fields have visible labels; status messages are announced to screen readers.
  • Charts carry a text summary, and the figures are also shown in tables.
  • Preview videos never play on their own, have player controls and a written description.
  • Text and controls meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio with the default theme, and pages reflow at 320 px width and 400% zoom.
  • Animations are disabled when the operating system asks for reduced motion.

Known limitations

  • Cartridge icons, screenshots and preview videos are provided by their authors. Preview videos have no captions; they contain gameplay without speech.
  • Installing over USB needs a browser with Web Serial (Chrome or Edge). Downloading a cartridge works in every browser.
  • Administrators can change the theme colours. A custom theme may lower contrast; the Setup page warns about it.
